#  Customer Behavior Analysis

##  Project Overview

An end-to-end **Customer Behavior Analysis** project focused on understanding customer purchasing patterns, spending behavior, product preferences, subscription activity, discounts, shipping choices, and customer segments.

The project uses **Excel, SQL, PostgreSQL, and Power BI** to transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, analysis, and interactive visualization.

## Tech Stack

*  Power BI – Interactive dashboard & visualization
*  PostgreSQL – Database management & SQL analysis
*  SQL– Business analysis and customer insights
*  Excel – Data cleaning & preprocessing
*  Power Query – Data transformation
*  DAX – Measures and KPIs

##  Dataset

* 3,900 customer records
* 19 attributes
*  Customer purchase and product information
*  Payment and subscription details
*  Discount and shipping information
*  Product review ratings
*  Previous purchase history
*  Customer demographic information

##  Key Analysis

*  Revenue comparison between male and female customers
*  Discount usage and customer spending behavior
*  Top-rated products
*  Standard vs. Express shipping spending
*  Subscriber vs. non-subscriber purchasing behavior
*  Products with the highest discount rates
   New, Returning, and Loyal customer segmentation
*  Top products within each category
*  Repeat buyers and subscription behavior
*  Revenue contribution by age group

##  Objective

The objective of this project is to demonstrate practical **Data Analyst skills** by analyzing customer behavior and converting raw transactional data into actionable business insights using SQL, PostgreSQL, and Power BI.

##  Skills Demonstrated

**Data Cleaning | SQL | PostgreSQL | Excel | Power Query | Power BI | DAX | Data Visualization | Customer Segmentation | Exploratory Data Analysis | Business Intelligence**
